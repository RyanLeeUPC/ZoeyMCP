      Credentials

      Every credential in the policy, the routes that use it, and what needs you. Connect signs in with an OAuth provider in a new tab, and you paste back the address it lands on (or, for a credential registered with the portal's callback address, the provider sends it back itself); the vault stores the grant after you confirm with your passkey.         Sessions

        Every signed-in browser. A session ends after 15 idle minutes, 4 hours in all, at sign-out, or when the vault restarts.

          Passkeys

          Every passkey that can sign in to this vault. A passkey works on the host name it was enrolled on. Each change asks for a passkey first. The last passkey can't be removed here: enrol another first, or use zoeymcp setup-code --reset.

            Secrets

            Every secret in the vault's store, by name, and what it may be used for. The vault never shows a value, here or anywhere else: to change one, enter it again. Each change asks for a passkey first. A credential's tokens from Connect are the vault's own and aren't changed here.

              Ledger

              The vault's copy of the gateway's approval ledger, newest first. Every row is as the gateway reported it. A call shows its sends from the SMTP connectors' journals, and a send whose call the copy doesn't hold is listed below it. A call's arguments are only in the gateway's own ledger, as a keyed digest: check them with zoeymcp ledger check REF FILE in the gateway's container.
